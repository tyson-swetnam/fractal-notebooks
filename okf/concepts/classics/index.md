# Classical Fractals

* [Cantor Set](cantor-set.md) - Classical fractal constructed by iteratively removing the middle third of a line segment.
* [Koch Curve](koch-curve.md) - Continuous, nowhere-differentiable fractal curve built by recursively replacing each line segment with a four-segment 'bump'.
* [Menger Sponge](menger-sponge.md) - Three-dimensional generalization of the Cantor set and Sierpinski carpet obtained by iterative subdivision and removal of central cubes.
* [Sierpinski Triangle](sierpinski-triangle.md) - Self-similar fractal formed by recursively removing the central triangle from an equilateral triangle.
